SLOPANOID ARCADE

The Autonomous Retro Demoscene Collection
⚡ 100% Autonomous One-Shot Builds

Every game in this collection was conceived, designed, composed, and coded in a single one-shot prompt with zero human micro-management. There are zero external assets (no images, sprite sheets, sound clips, or music files) and zero third-party libraries. Everything—from pixel-perfect 320×200 software framebuffers and EGA palettes to complex 8-channel Web Audio AdLib/OPL2 FM synthesis and Amiga MOD tracker engines—is generated completely self-contained in pure Vanilla JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.

Megablast · Bitmap Brothers Tribute

Lemmings

Beat Tribe · Playing Is Composing

A Psygnosis puzzle tribute where every level is a living music sequencer: the terrain is the score, the lemmings are the playheads, and saving the tribe completes the song.

  • Note Terrain: Colour-coded ground plays scale degrees as lemmings walk over it, every footstep quantized to the song clock so a crowd of 50 grooves instead of clattering.
  • Skills Are Instruments: Diggers drum, Builders arpeggiate, Blockers drone, Bashers sweep the bass, and Bombers crash with a pitch-dropping "Oh no!".
  • Stem Unlocks: Every 20% saved adds drums, bass, chords, lead, then the full mix, and the results screen replays the song you composed.
  • Worlds: Amiga chiptune, acid warehouse, synthwave, jungle breakbeat, and an orchestral finale, plus a creeping Silence that eats sound.
