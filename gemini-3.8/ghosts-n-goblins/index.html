HOW TO PLAY & TACTICS

  • A / D or ← / →: Walk Left / Right
  • W or ↑: Aim Upward / Climb Ladders
  • S or ↓: Crouch / Climb Down
  • SPACE or Z: Jump (Double-jump in Boxer Shorts!)
  • J or X or Left-Click: Attack / Throw Weapon (Hold to charge Golden Magic!)
  • E or C or Right-Click:
    • In Steel/Golden Armor: TACTICAL EJECT! Drops a heavy crushing Anvil & spring-pad!
    • In Boxer Shorts: SOUL POSSESSION! Possess monsters for 8 seconds!
  • 1 to 6: Quick Warp to any stage!
